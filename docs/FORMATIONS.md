# Formations

In the Missions tab, every flight that starts in the air and every ground group has a **Formation** and a **Spacing**.
The leader stands on the point you placed (or the first route point), facing the way the group moves. Spacing is
the distance in metres between neighbours; leave it empty for the style's usual distance.

## Air (air starts only; runway and parking starts keep the airfield's own layout)

| Style | Shape |
|---|---|
| As before | The staggered trail the mission maker always used: everyone behind the leader, alternately left and right, 120 m apart. |
| Vic | A V with the leader at the point. |
| Finger-four | Pairs: leader with a wingman back right, the second pair back left. Further fours follow behind. |
| Line astern | One behind the other. |
| Line abreast | Side by side. |
| Echelon left / right | A diagonal line stepping back to one side. |

## Ground

| Style | Shape |
|---|---|
| As before | A column when the group has a route, a line abreast when it stands still (30 m / 45 m). |
| Line abreast | Side by side across the heading. |
| Column / road march | One behind the other. |
| Wedge | An arrowhead with the leader at the front. |
| Echelon left / right | A diagonal line stepping back to one side. |
| Ring | All-round defence: the leader in the middle, the rest on a circle with a full spacing between neighbours. |
| Cluster | A tight square block at half the spacing. |
| Dispersed | Scattered one to three spacings from the leader. The scatter is the same every time you write the mission. |

A ground unit that would stand in the sea is pulled back toward its leader until it is on land.
